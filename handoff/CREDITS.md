## The AI team (ranked, no filter)

### 1. DeepSeek
The MVP. Parsed a raw btsnoop log like it was bedtime reading, found the
Scroll sequence, found the opcodes, and stayed in the chat for hours
without crying about tokens.
**Verdict:** Would hire again.

### 2. Cursor AI
Doesn't talk much. Just writes code, moves files, and fixes things while
you're not looking. Quiet, competent, slightly suspicious.
**Verdict:** The strong silent type of the group.

### 3. Google Studio AI
Took the interface from "Windows 95 vibes" to "wait, this is actually nice."
Was about to make the app run on web, PC, tablet, and mobile before I told
it to sit down.
**Verdict:** Overachiever. Saved for V2.

### 4. GitHub Copilot
Found the color/brightness split that unlocked the whole protocol. Genius
move. Then spent the next hour asking me to "just edit line 47" like I'm
supposed to know where line 47 is.
**Verdict:** Best detective. Worst bedside manner.

### 5. Gemini
You can talk to it for hours. It will hold your hand, nod politely, and

**Verdict:** Great therapist. Mid developer.

### 6. Claude
Built the very first version. Then ran out of tokens faster than I run out

### 7. Everyone else (Kimi, Meta AI, ChatGPT, etc.)

## The one honest truth

None of them would have found anything without the btsnoop log, the
hardware tests, and the user saying "no, that's wrong, try again."
Every AI here built on the back of a human who kept correcting them.

**Rank #0 belongs to the user.**
