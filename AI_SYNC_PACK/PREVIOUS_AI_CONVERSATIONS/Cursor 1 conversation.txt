## Can it be done?

**Yes for a free Windows remote for your three MR Star clones.** That part is already proven: Bleak write to `fff3`, color and brightness as two packets, physical remote as the ground truth.

- Hardcoded MACs are fine for your desk. They are not a product for other people’s strips.
